Businesses in China.

How has U.S. and European business presence in China changed over time?

Annual histories, 2000-2024 where reported. China is the host in every chart. The main graphs track U.S., EU, German, UK and Norwegian business presence in China over time—not U.S. businesses located in Europe. Sparse company-count benchmarks are secondary.

Parent companies, affiliate enterprises and physical branches are different counting units. Annual affiliate/enterprise counts are not an annual census of unique parent companies or physical offices.

Company counts, affiliate entities and branch locations are distinct. U.S. parents and ultimately EU-controlled enterprises are not matched ownership populations; BEA and Eurostat levels must not be used to rank otherwise comparable business presence.

China is the host in every chart. Germany is shown separately from the overlapping EU27 aggregate. Historical EU membership, UK and Norwegian populations remain distinct; no all-Europe total is calculated.
