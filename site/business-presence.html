The full history has four distinct populations: nonbank above $3m in 2000-01; nonbank above $10m in 2002-06; including banks above $15m in 2007-11; including banks above $20m in 2012-24. The earlier levels are not joined to the current chart as a single comparable trend. Survey benchmarks also update coverage; a change is not necessarily a new firm or an exit. Macau is not supplied by these count tables and is not assigned zero.
