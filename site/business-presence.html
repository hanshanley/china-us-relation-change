Business ties.

How has the business presence linking the United States, China and Europe changed?

Parent companies, affiliate enterprises and physical branches are different counting units. This page covers both U.S. companies in China versus Europe and EU-controlled enterprises in China, without pretending those sources have matched populations.

Company counts, affiliate entities and branch locations are distinct. U.S. parents and ultimately EU-controlled enterprises are not matched ownership populations; BEA and Eurostat levels must not be used to rank otherwise comparable business presence.

China excludes Hong Kong; Macau is separately identified by the sources. BEA Europe is broader than EU27 and includes the UK, Switzerland, Russia and other non-EU countries. Eurostat uses fixed EU27_2020 membership, excluding the UK.

These are business-presence stocks, not annual openings, closures, investment flows or a census of office locations. No current count of all American-owned businesses in China is established by this publication.

What the counts establish

Latest verified benchmark parent counts: China 1,134 in 2019; Europe 2,701 in 2019. The separate annual majority-owned affiliate counts are China 1,950 in 2023 and Europe 17,956 in 2023. Do not substitute one counting unit for the other.

As checked October 22, 2026, BEA's latest activity release covers 2023. The published schedule puts the 2024 activity release on November 20, 2026; no 2024 value is inferred.

Thresholds are nominal dollars, not inflation-adjusted. Apparent growth across survey benchmarks can reflect improved coverage. Benchmark parent observations remain isolated; annual affiliate lines break at the 2004, 2009, 2014 and 2019 regime boundaries. Eurostat changes methodology in 2021.
