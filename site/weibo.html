This is a frozen aggregate snapshot, not a live Weibo feed. Current identity evidence does not prove current activity. Zero recovery does not mean zero posting; unknown accounts are not verified absences. Historical licensing cutoffs are respected. Counts are not audience size, popularity or evidence of a causal link to trade or travel.

The sample includes explicitly labelled Chinese editions and U.S.-based Chinese-language media. Institutional identity is not proof of direct U.S.-newsroom operation or a continuing license; per-account relationships and attribution exclusions are retained in the aggregate JSON.

Only aggregate counts and public institutional account metadata are published. No post text, private corpus records or engagement rankings are included. Sources and requested collection windows are identified in the complete aggregate JSON.
