Mainland China,
Hong Kong and Macau.

Mainland China, Hong Kong and Macau, with a fixed colour for each geography. Taiwan is excluded from these comparisons. No regional totals are constructed.

Annual history from 2000 where verified. Series can end in different years. An asterisk identifies an unavailable series, not zero. Log views omit true zeros and never replace them.

Macau goods use customs accounting; the other goods lines use BEA balance-of-payments accounting. The dashed Macau line is not an identical accounting measure. Population-definition breaks remain gaps.

All figures are available as 3000 × 1800 PNGs and SVGs, with both publication backgrounds.

Source access and reuse restrictions
  • IIE Open Doors: IIE automatic retrieval is paused: its terms prohibit automated downloading and analysis. Previously reviewed IIE-hosted observations are retained, not newly checked. Historical aggregates are independently reviewed in government republications; exact retrieved URLs, checksums, vintages and dates remain on those rows. Separate direct retrieval and reuse permission has not been verified; public access is not commercial-use clearance. Source terms
  • Macao Government Tourism Office / DSEC: MGTO site terms limit use to noncommercial purposes. Commercial reuse permission has not been verified; these exports do not grant publication rights. Source terms
