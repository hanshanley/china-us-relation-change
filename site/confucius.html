Definitions & sources

Snapshots retain their original reference dates, including year-only and month-only observations. A report upload, webpage modification or retrieval in a later year does not make an old count current. No annual observations or national operating totals are inferred from partial inventories.

Successor counts retain NAS attribution and its broader definition of replacement. A proposal, a continuing partner relationship and a host transfer are not automatically completed rebrands. The supporting workbook has 29 R-coded rows, including prospective or ambiguous descriptions; these flags are retained, not substituted for a current operating total. Schools and districts are not interchangeable counting units.

GAO selected facts use an explicitly identified, versioned transcription; its checksum is not a checksum of the publisher PDF. Other numerical inputs retain publisher-response checksums. Only factual aggregates and selected factual record fields are redistributed, not report text or artwork.
