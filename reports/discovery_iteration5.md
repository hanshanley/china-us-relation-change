## Recovering posts without weakening identity checks

The strict live-feed gate correctly rejects the numeric profile title against
the registered historical NPR name. Numeric placeholders were deliberately
not added as publisher aliases.

Two named-owner captures from
[January 2022](https://web.archive.org/web/20220122230335id_/https://weibo.com/nprshanghai)
and November 2023 accepted 14 and 15 in-window source records. Overlap
reconciles these to **15 distinct NPR posts**, not 29.

Native detail enrichment was then limited to those already archive-verified
post IDs. Each accepted detail independently matched both the post ID and NPR
author UID, and its publication minute matched the archived record. The
existing normalized import path preserved source versions and separate
capture/retrieval observations; the live profile-name rule was not relaxed.

Twelve native details were recovered. Three cross-host/non-HTTPS redirects
were not followed, leaving those posts as explicit previews. NPR's resulting
local corpus has **12 source-full accessible bodies and three truncated
records**. Source-full still does not certify completeness against the
original Weibo text. Raw responses and all post text remain private.
