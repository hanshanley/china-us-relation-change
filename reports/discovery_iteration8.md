supplies the actual UID and publication name. That source was a locator
lead; the permitted current profile independently confirms the publication
identity. No blocked live Weibo endpoint was fetched, and a separate Sina
blog ID was not assumed to be the Weibo UID.

[Contemporaneous May 2004 launch coverage](https://eladies.sina.com.cn/2004-05-28/96212.html)
explicitly identifies American Men's Health and its Chinese copyright
partner. It distinguishes the 2003 cooperation intent from the formal
May 2004 launch. The actual global
[Men's Health editorial-information page](https://www.menshealth.com/about/a29134632/about-mens-health/)
was separately retrieved from the publication's own linked About route.
Staff biographies, models, health practitioners, a parent-group app and
third-party image credits were not counted as publication accounts.

The corporate site's robots request returned HTTP 403, so no blocked
corporate-directory page was fetched.
The edition's historical license relationship and currently observable
institutional identity do **not** independently audit current contracts,
legal account ownership, uninterrupted licensing or U.S.-newsroom staffing.
No unrelated publisher's license-termination boundary was transferred to
this magazine by assumption.
