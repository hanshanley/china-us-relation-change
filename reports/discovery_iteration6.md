the NPR checkpoint rather than closing the inventory. The publication window

## Nineteen additional identities, not nineteen independent newsrooms

outlet/brand groups**, with **91 reviewed outlet/candidate rows and 219
evidence records**. Eighteen additions are seventeen PR Newswire channels
and one Business Wire-branded Chinese distribution channel; another is

The two wire brands are **commercial news-release services**; the totals must not

**eleven positive domains and 989 unknowns**. The source roster is a digital
reputation ranking, not a traffic ranking or corporate-ownership audit.

### Business Wire: independent authorized distribution, not direct ownership

The regional [contact page](https://www.businesswirechina.com/zh/about/contact.html)
directly embeds UID `2676312451`. Its
[public institutional profile](https://www.sina.cn/media/2676312451)
explicitly identifies Business Wire China's official account.

Crucially, the portal's [own operator statement](https://www.businesswirechina.com/en/about/about_us.html)
names **Interfax News Service Limited**, an independent authorized
distributor, and expressly disclaims legal agency, partnership and joint
venture with Business Wire. The inventory preserves that distinction;
the account is not asserted to be directly owned or staffed by a U.S.
newsroom, and Interfax is not reclassified as a U.S. publisher.

Business Wire's own [2014 corporate milestone document](https://mms.businesswire.com/media/20140930005849/en/434193/1/BWSOC930_timeline_2014v6.pdf)
independently establishes its San Francisco origin and historical
distribution agreements including Interfax. That historical evidence does
not independently audit today's contract, account ownership or license
continuity. It corroborates the brand relationship, not direct U.S. control.

## Knowledge@Wharton: positive historical presence, unresolved profile locator

Wharton's [own 2014 media report](https://news.wharton.upenn.edu/wp-content/uploads/2012/06/Wharton-Global-Forum-Beijing_Coverage-Wrap-Up-Report_20140702.pdf)
explicitly identifies Knowledge@Wharton's Weibo account in the contents and
on page 131. Pages 131-132 contain screenshots of its posts. This is
**positive historical account-presence evidence**, not merely a matching
name in unrelated coverage.

No stable profile URL or UID was visible on those pages, and an independent
inspection of all PDF hyperlinks did not expose an identity locator. The
2014 screenshots predate the corpus window. They are not imported as
2020-2026 posts, nor are their old engagement figures current measurements.
No guessed `/n/` address or parent-school account is inserted into the
resolved identity total.

The actual current U.S. publication portal was readable but exposed no
Weibo link in this bounded check. Wharton's
[2005 publication announcement](https://magazine.wharton.upenn.edu/issues/summer-2005/wharton-now-24/)
documents the Chinese edition and its Shanghai publication operation;
its [2019 retrospective](https://magazine.wharton.upenn.edu/issues/spring-summer-2019/20-years-of-knowledge-wharton/)
separately explains the global publication's evolution.

Exact September 2014 and January 2016 captures of
`www.knowledgeatwharton.com.cn` were independently retrieved and identified
as the actual Chinese publication, including Penn/Wharton ownership and
global-language links. No qualifying Weibo locator appeared in those two
homepages. The differently spelled domain printed in the 2005 announcement
returned no index rows in the bounded historical lookup; that is not
account-absence evidence. Present-day control of an old Chinese domain is
not assumed from historical publisher use.

An additional October 2012 capture used the older Chinese publisher
template, but also exposed no qualifying account locator. It is retained
as a separate dated check rather than repeated as a new current-page check.
