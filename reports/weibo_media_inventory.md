## Method and remaining gaps

Discovery prioritizes publisher-owned outbound links, explicit platform certification naming the outlet, and first-hand publisher confirmation. Search results and matching logos alone do not confirm officialness.

Collection uses the pilot-verified public Sina syndication feed, linked detail pages, and selected public Wayback profile snapshots, respecting robots rules and access failures. No Weibo gates are bypassed. Detail text is labeled source_full: the complete accessible syndicated body, not a guarantee of fidelity to an inaccessible original. Feed previews remain truncated in provenance. Reposts cannot reliably be identified on this source.

An attribution end excludes posts on or after that Asia/Shanghai date across live, archive and import routes, even when a UID retains the same name. These are evidence-backed collection cutoffs, not necessarily exact contract-termination dates; see each account's evidence and notes. An unestablished cutoff does not prove a continuing U.S. license.

No feed-history pagination has been demonstrated. Archive index hits alone are not recovered posts. Authorized/manual/archive JSONL imports can extend coverage, retaining publication versus capture dates. Resolve unknown UIDs before attempting account-specific syndication retrieval. No claim is made that any outlet lacking evidence never used Weibo.

Corpus text and raw responses are local-only by default. Public exports omit posts without explicit redistribution permission and list omissions. No third-party post text is reproduced in this report.
