## Knowledge at Wharton: a publication account, not a borrowed school profile

The previous checkpoint had positive first-party evidence that
Knowledge@Wharton used Weibo, but no stable locator. Wharton's
[2014 Beijing Forum media report](https://news.wharton.upenn.edu/wp-content/uploads/2012/06/Wharton-Global-Forum-Beijing_Coverage-Wrap-Up-Report_20140702.pdf)
explicitly identifies its publication's account and reproduces June 2014
posts. Reinspecting the original screenshot at higher resolution confirmed
that the crop contains post bodies, not a usable owner nickname or URL;
the mentioned organizations and old short links were not mistaken for
the account owner.

New bounded archive discovery found an actual captured handle,
**`whartonckw`**. The
[May 2011 profile](https://web.archive.org/web/20110504215454id_/http://weibo.com:80/whartonckw)
explicitly names **沃顿知识在线官方微博** in the owner's own certification
header. Its canonical `scope.defaulturl` and `scope.globalurl`, together
with name-bearing `scope.nickname` and `scope.setGroup`, all bind the owner
to **UID `1749002512`**. Recommended-account avatars and viewer variables
were not used.

A separately retrieved
[December 2013 profile](https://web.archive.org/web/20131228002534id_/http://weibo.com:80/whartonckw)
independently gives the same `$CONFIG` owner UID and publisher name.
Its five recognized old posts predate the corpus window and were not
imported as 2020-2026 material.

The [current public institutional profile](https://www.sina.cn/media/1749002512)
retains the publication name, blue institutional badge and exact
publication-specific official descriptor. The
[Chinese publication's own historical homepage](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908002705id_/http://www.knowledgeatwharton.com.cn/)
separately establishes the Penn/Wharton publishing relationship and
Chinese-language edition. This is not a parent university, school, alumnus,
staff member or similarly named training provider counted as a magazine.

The inventory therefore adds **one account and one publisher/brand group**.
Its Chinese-language operation is separately labeled, without asserting
U.S.-newsroom staffing or independently audited legal account control.
Observable identity does not imply posting activity today: visible native
feed records include 2023-2024 publication dates. The 2014 screenshot's
read counts are not current followers or engagement.

The full resolved inventory is now **72 account identities across 49
publisher/brand groups**, with **91 reviewed outlet/candidate rows and
225 structured evidence records**. Knowledge at Wharton's already
registered canonical domain is supplementary, so the selected 1,000-domain
cohort remains **eleven positive domains and 989 unknowns**, not verified
negatives. Prefix-neighbor accounts were not automatically accepted.

## Popular Science: remove an unsupported cutoff, not an identity safeguard

The current display **PS大众科学** was previously held because it differed
from the registered historical name. Direct review of the owner's own
institutional badge and descriptor, rather than the display alone,
establishes an explicit U.S. Popular Science Chinese-edition identity.
A separately retrieved
[June 2026 native post](https://www.sina.cn/news/detail/5308203458888978.html)
passes exact post-ID and JSON-LD author-UID `1925964485` checks and displays
the same explicit institutional certification.

The current alias is therefore independently supported; generic numeric
placeholders, similar magazine names and follower recommendations remain
unacceptable identity substitutes. The stable account ID is preserved,
and its current link uses the numeric UID. This is **not another account**
added to inflate the inventory.

Importantly, the
[October 8, 2014 publisher-domain notice](https://web.archive.org/web/20141222170838id_/http://www.popscichina.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/about_stop_publishing.pdf)
says Wanhua Media stops **distribution from the November issue**. It does
not establish U.S.-brand license termination or cessation of the account.
The previous exploratory November 2014 attribution boundary was therefore
too broad and is withdrawn. An actual contract-termination notice, such
as those underpinning other brands' cutoffs, is a different kind of
evidence; those other boundaries are unchanged.

This reassessment does **not** establish uninterrupted licensing, current
legal ownership or U.S. authorship of the Chinese channel's output. Those
limits remain explicit. Certification seen on a current rendering is
observed at retrieval time; it is not backdated to the native post's
publication date.

The newly discovered `popsci.com.cn` site was also checked alongside the
actual U.S. publisher root. Similar translated branding and article text,
without an independently established publisher relationship, did not
cause that domain to be promoted as an authorized Chinese edition. The
account determination rests on the historically publisher-proven UID and
explicit current owner-scoped certification, not an assumed branded mirror.

The collector then recovered **100 eligible posts** from the verified
