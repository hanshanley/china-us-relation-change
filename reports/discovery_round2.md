## A stable UID does not guarantee a stable publisher

TechCrunch is the important counterexample. The publisher's January 2019
Chinese homepage directly linked `tcchinese`, and an April 2019 profile binds
that handle to UID `3561737261`. TechNode's own about page dates the exclusive
TechCrunch partnership to **2013-2019**.

The UID now serves **动点出海**, described as a TechNode outbound-business
vertical. Current posts must not be relabeled as TechCrunch simply because
the numeric UID stayed the same. The historical registry display name is
retained, and the collector's exact profile-name validation rejects the
current rebranded timeline. No independent TechNode feed text is imported
as TechCrunch in this round.

Vogue's website account illustrates a different case: the publisher-linked
`voguewebsite` resolves to UID `1655526104`, now VogueFilm, and its current
institutional profile explicitly identifies the Vogue magazine website and
official film channel. Both the old publisher link and current identity are
retained rather than relying on a display-name match alone.
